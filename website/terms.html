Using the service

Your content

Your recordings, transcripts and memory belong to you. You give the service permission to process them only to provide the features you use (note sync, search and Ask) and to store them as you chose. Transcription and summaries happen on your phone, or with the provider key you enter. We do not sell your content or use it to train models.

Accounts and keys

  • Keep your password and API keys secret. You are responsible for what is done with them; revoke a key or sign a device out from Account if one is lost.

  • Limits (such as questions per day and sync size) apply and are enforced by the server.
