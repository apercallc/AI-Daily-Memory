01 · What it is

A memory system, not a transcription app.

Recording is the means. The product is recall you can trust: every decision, action item, and commitment it shows you carries a timestamp and a link back to the exact moment it came from.

CAPTURE

The phone remembers

A foreground service records your day in short, encrypted segments — offline, crash-resistant, with an honest health panel that never claims capture is fine when it isn't.

UNDERSTAND

Speech becomes structure

When you connect an account, speech is transcribed and lifted into decisions, actions, commitments, ideas, and questions — each citing its transcript evidence.

REMEMBER

The day comes back to you

A daily report for your memory day: what was decided, what you took on, what others promised — with timestamps, so you can check any claim against the source.

04 · Privacy

Built like it's recording your life. Because it is.

This app can capture real conversations with real people in them, so privacy is a product requirement here — not a settings page.

1

Recording is always visible

A persistent notification and on-screen indicator exist whenever capture runs. Pause or stop from the phone, the watch, or the notification itself. Hiding recording state is against this product's own rules.

2

Audio is encrypted on your phone

Each segment is sealed with AES-256-GCM under keys held in the Android Keystore, and excluded from backups and device transfers.

3

Nothing is retained server-side by default

In "On this phone" mode, data is not kept on our server after processing. When a provider is enabled, speech clips are sent transiently for transcription and the transcript is sealed to your phone's key. Today both providers are disabled by default, so uploads are refused until an operator enables them.

4

Cloud processing is temporary

When enabled, audio clips leave the phone for processing; temporary copies are deleted after the verified transcript is stored or the job reaches terminal failure. The phone keeps the original until the transcript is stored locally.

5

Transcripts are sealed to your key

In phone storage modes, transcript lines are encrypted to your phone's own key before delivery. The server holds only a temporary encrypted copy while waiting for your phone to acknowledge receipt (maximum 7 days).

6

Deletion is honest

Stop asks Keep or Delete. Deleting removes that session's audio and derived data. We say plainly what deletion does — and what it can't, like physical flash erasure — instead of over-promising.

Three storage modes

Where your memory lives is your call, per mode. Switchable in settings.

On this phone Default

Long-term memory stays on your phone. If cloud processing is enabled, clips are sent temporarily for transcription and the transcript is sealed back to your phone's key. Today processing is disabled by default.

→ transient processing when enabled; no server-side memory retained
Reports in my cloud account

Designed to retain daily reports and structured items server-side while keeping full transcripts on your phone. This mode is a product target; report retention for this mode is not implemented yet.

→ not available yet
Full cloud

Server-held transcripts and memory for API/MCP access. This requires an explicit choice; provider stages are disabled by default.

→ readable server-side, by choice and only when enabled

07 · Questions

Asked and answered.

Q1Is this just another transcription app?+
No. Recording and transcription are the means; the product is a memory you can query and trust. The difference is evidence: every decision, action item, or commitment it extracts links back to the timestamped moment it came from, and your corrections never touch the original.
Q2Can it record people without them knowing?+
It's not built for that, and it isn't designed to hide recording state — a persistent notification and on-screen indicator are product requirements, and pausing is always one tap away from the phone, the watch, or the notification. Recording laws vary by place and situation; follow yours.
Q3Where does my audio actually live?+
Audio is stored on your phone by default, encrypted with keys in the Android Keystore, and excluded from backups. If an account is connected and a provider is enabled, speech clips may be sent transiently for transcription even in "On this phone" mode; that mode means the server doesn't keep memory after processing. Today both providers are disabled by default, so uploads are refused. Raw audio is not kept as an archive.
Q4Do I need an account for it to be useful?+
No. The phone records, organizes sessions, tracks capture health, and runs speech analysis entirely offline. An account only matters when you want processing, reports, or access from other tools — and you can connect one later, with recording from before staying on the phone unless you say otherwise.
Q5Does it work offline?+
Capture is offline-first by design: recording never depends on the network or a server. Uploads happen later, on Wi-Fi, while charging. If the connection drops mid-session, nothing is lost — the queue waits.
Q6What about my watch?+
The Wear OS app is a controller: start, pause, resume, stop, highlight, or drop a Quick Note from your wrist. It always shows what the phone has acknowledged — never an assumed success. The watch never records on its own.
Q7What will it cost?+
Private testing is free. When it's public there will be a subscription — all-day transcription and AI processing have real per-user costs — with the honest details (free tier, limits, what's metered) published before anyone is asked to pay.
Q8Can I delete everything?+
Yes. Stopping a session asks Keep or Delete; deleting removes that session's audio and its derived data. Retention is a setting, and full account deletion — including derived memory — is planned as a right, not an upsell.
