01 · What it is

Come back to what mattered.

The goal is to make a conversation useful long after it ends: find a decision, check who promised what, and return to the evidence. Capture is the current focus; daily reports and natural-language recall are still in development.

CAPTURE

The phone remembers

The Android build records short, encrypted audio segments without a network connection. Real-device reliability and endurance checks are still outstanding.

UNDERSTAND

Speech becomes structure

The phone turns speech into text with a free model it downloads once (English), then deletes the audio. Summaries and action items are optional and use your own AI key.

REMEMBER

The day comes back to you

The planned daily report brings decisions and follow-ups together with their source timestamps. The illustration below shows the direction, not a shipped feature.

04 · Privacy

Built like it's recording your life. Because it is.

This app can capture real conversations with real people in them, so privacy is a product requirement here — not a settings page.

1

Recording is always visible

A persistent notification and on-screen indicator exist whenever capture runs. Pause or stop from the phone, the watch, or the notification itself. Hiding recording state is against this product's own rules.

2

Audio is encrypted on your phone

Each segment is sealed with AES-256-GCM under keys held in the Android Keystore, and excluded from backups and device transfers.

3

Audio stays on your phone by default

With on-phone transcription, speech is turned into text on the phone and the audio is deleted afterwards. The app does not upload audio to our server. If you enable your own transcription service, the phone sends audio directly to that provider.

4

Your own keys stay yours

If you add a transcription or AI key, the phone talks to that provider directly and the key is stored sealed on the phone. It never passes through our server. Summaries send transcript text; a transcription key sends audio. Both are off until you turn them on.

5

Sync is a choice, and it is notes only

With an account, the phone sends each day's summary and items. Full transcripts are sent only if you switch that on. Delete a session on the phone and the next sync removes it from the server.

6

Deletion is honest

Stop asks Keep or Delete. Deleting removes that session's audio and derived data. We say plainly what deletion does — and what it can't, like physical flash erasure — instead of over-promising.

Where your notes live

Your choice, changeable in settings.

On this phone Default

By default, audio, transcripts and summaries stay on the phone. No account or server sync is needed. A transcription or AI service you choose receives the data needed for that service directly from your phone.

07 · Questions

Asked and answered.

Q1Is this just another transcription app?+
No. Recording and transcription are the means; the product is a memory you can query and trust. The difference is evidence: every decision, action item, or commitment it extracts links back to the timestamped moment it came from, and your corrections never touch the original.
Q2Can it record people without them knowing?+
It's not built for that, and it isn't designed to hide recording state — a persistent notification and on-screen indicator are product requirements, and pausing is always one tap away from the phone, the watch, or the notification. Recording laws vary by place and situation; follow yours.
Q3Where does my audio actually live?+
On your phone only. It is encrypted with keys in the Android Keystore, excluded from backups, and deleted once it has been turned into text. It is never uploaded to our server. The one exception is yours to make: if you enter your own transcription key, the phone sends audio to that provider directly.
Q4Do I need an account for it to be useful?+
No. The phone records, organizes sessions, tracks capture health, and runs speech analysis entirely offline. An account only matters when you want your notes on the web or in other tools — and you can connect one later, with notes from before staying on the phone unless you say otherwise.
Q5Does it work offline?+
Capture is offline-first by design: recording never depends on the network or a server. Transcription runs on the phone while it charges, and note sync waits for a connection. The one-time speech model download (about 40 MB) needs a connection; recording does not.
Q6What about my watch?+
The Wear OS app is a controller: start, pause, resume, stop, highlight, or drop a Quick Note from your wrist. It always shows what the phone has acknowledged — never an assumed success. The watch never records on its own.
Q7What will it cost?+
Nothing. The app, on-phone transcription, sync, the web app and the API are free. Summaries use an AI key you supply, so any cost there is between you and that provider. If hosting costs ever force a change, it will be announced well before anything is charged.
Q8Can I delete everything?+
Yes. Stopping a session asks Keep or Delete; deleting removes that session's audio and its derived data, and a later sync removes the server-side copy of that session's memory. If you have a server account, deleting it (DELETE /v1/me) removes your server-side data and your storage prefix, and the call is retried if cleanup is incomplete. You can also download everything the server holds as one file from Account.
